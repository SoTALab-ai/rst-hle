conflicting instruction above.
Preserve the selected scientific goal and grading requirement. The goal determines the answer's
form and length; do not force it into a short quantitative result. Keep qualitative, classification,
construction, algorithm and proof obligations when they are part of the locked objective.
The reference proof may follow the source paper's derivation. A step may be justified by faithfully
applying a cited argument of the paper (theorem/equation/page locator) after checking that its
hypotheses hold for the locked instance; you need not re-derive textbook details. Never cite the
paper for a claim it does not make, and state explicitly where you adapted or specialized it.
Do not simplify the model, drop couplings or shrink parameters to make verification easier.
Mask selection: the bottleneck is the source mechanism's key inference or its justified counterpart
in the locked adapted construction. mask.hidden_information must list that inference AND every scaffold that would give it
away: derived representations, ansatzes, effective operators or loads, Schur complements, special
normalizations, auxiliary functions, named theorems or ready-to-apply identities. Other derivable
intermediate steps may stay hidden as well. mask.retained_information contains the locked model's
primitive objects and definitions, model laws, given data, quantifiers, conventions
that affect the answer, and the requested output. In recoverability, cite where the paper derives
the hidden inference, checking its hypotheses against these retained premises; justify adapted steps explicitly.
