PAPER HARD-STEP MODE (correctness_basis=paper_grounded). These rules take precedence over any
conflicting instruction above.
Judge the inference demanded by the selected scientific goal, irrespective of answer length or
quantitative form. For a proof goal, the stated proposition is the task; its presence alone does
not reveal the requested argument. A conclusion-only locked goal cannot be changed into a proof.
hidden and not_answer_only stay strict: fail if the question supplies the selected inference, an
equivalent scaffold or a recipe that gives it away, or if only the final answer slot is masked.
recoverable: return pass when the source paper (or the reference proof) derives the hidden inference
from the premises retained in the literal question. Return fail ONLY with a concrete witness: a
missing primitive premise that the derivation needs, or two admissible instances consistent with
the literal question that have different targets. Otherwise return uncertain. Difficulty of
recovery is never a failure.
Apply the same standard-background rule as the scientific audit: the named theory, physical regime
and approximation are part of the public inputs. Definitions/laws of that model and their derived
consequences need not be supplied as intermediate formulas. Before alleging non-recoverability,
check legitimate alternative derivations, including conjugate-color transport and antiquark matching
for QCD small-x tasks. An arbitrary negative-x extension is not an admissible witness unless it
obeys that same model's operator and transport laws. State the independent freedom that survives
those constraints. A gap in one reference route or inability to establish a route is uncertainty,
not proof that the task lacks inputs. A simpler valid problem is allowed; do not demand the author's
route or restore a subjective depth veto.
selection_supported and necessary: give your honest assessment with a concrete witness. They are
recorded as difficulty hypotheses only; the target solver is measured directly.
