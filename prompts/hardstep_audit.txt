PAPER HARD-STEP MODE (correctness_basis=paper_grounded). These rules take precedence over any
conflicting instruction above, including "any uncertainty in correctness should set valid=false".
Do not reject a goal because its natural answer is long, qualitative, a theorem, construction or
algorithm. Check that its literal requested obligations and grading requirement agree with the
locked scientific goal. Proof or noncanonical-witness validity is not final-answer equivalence.
Set self_contained=false or uniquely_answerable=false ONLY with a concrete witness: an undefined
symbol or object, a missing primitive datum that the derivation actually needs and that is not
standard background in the field, or two admissible readings of the literal question with different
answers (state both). "Hard to derive", "the solver may not find the step" or "the derivation is not
given" are NOT missing information; deliberately hidden derivable steps are the intended task.
Read the named theory, approximation and physical regime as part of the question. Standard field
definitions and laws, and consequences derived from them, need not be written as supplied formulas.
Before alleging missing inputs, try legitimate routes within that model, including routes different
from the reference proof. For example, in a QCD small-x question, examine conjugate color transport
and the antiquark matching derivation before demanding an explicitly supplied negative-x extension.
An arbitrary extension is not an admissible counterexample unless it obeys the stated QCD model,
operator definitions and approximation. Identify which genuinely independent choice remains free
after those constraints; an omitted intermediate equation or a gap in one proposed route is not
by itself evidence that the question is underdetermined. If that has not been established, record
the precise uncertainty instead of rejecting. A valid shorter solution is allowed even if the
source objective was harder; difficulty is measured by GLM, not this correctness review.
Set valid=false or answer_supported=false only for a concrete error. A step faithfully taken from the
cited paper with its hypotheses satisfied supports the answer. If you could not fully verify the
answer, keep valid=true and say exactly what you could not verify in reason.
Leakage stays strict: if the question supplies the hidden inference or an equivalent scaffold
(helper definition, ansatz, effective quantity, named theorem, ready-to-apply identity), set
no_shortcut=false and name it. substantive_delta and no_shortcut are recorded; depth is measured
by real solver trials.
